### 1. Proven High-Altitude Thru-Hikes & Sovereign Solo High-Pass Expeditions
* **Manaslu Circuit (177 km / 110 mi · +9,500m ascent)** — *Nepal Himalaya (Solo & Autonomous)*  
  Remote high-altitude expedition circumnavigating Mt. Manaslu (8,163m). Traverses subtropical river gorges, Tibetan-border Buddhist enclaves (Samagaon, Samdo), glaciated moraines, and the crux alpine crossing of **Larkya La Pass (5,106m / 16,752 ft)** in sub-zero alpine conditions before dropping into the Marsyangdi valley.
* **Tilicho Lake Circuit via Mesokanta Pass (160 km / 100 mi · Technical High Pass)** — *Annapurna Alpine, Nepal (Solo & Autonomous)*  
  High-alpine crossing bypassing the standard teahouse routes to reach the frozen waters of **Tilicho Lake (4,919m / 16,138 ft)**, followed by a non-teahouse off-trail alpine traverse over the formidable **Mesokanto La / Mesokanta Pass (5,121m / 16,797 ft)**. Involves steep scree couloirs, wilderness bivouacs, and technical descent into Mustang's Kali Gandaki valley.
* **Upper Mustang Circuit (140 km / 87 mi · Rain Shadow High Plateau)** — *Trans-Himalaya, Nepal (Solo & Autonomous)*  
  High-desert expedition into the historic, restricted Kingdom of Lo (Lo Manthang) bordering Tibet. Crossing multiple high passes including **Chogo La (4,280m / 14,042 ft)** and **Nyi La (4,010m / 13,156 ft)** across windswept canyons, eroded ochre cliffs, and arid, sub-zero high-plateau terrain.
* **Gosaikunda Circuit (120 km / 75 mi · +7,200m ascent)** — *Langtang to Helambu, Nepal (Solo & Autonomous)*  
  High-angle wilderness traverse linking the Langtang canyon with the sacred high-altitude glacial tarns of **Gosaikunda (4,380m / 14,370 ft)**, crossing **Laurebina Pass / Lauribinayak La (4,610m / 15,125 ft)** before navigating the remote high ridge crests of Helambu.
* **Wind River Range Continental Divide Wilderness Thru-Hike (90+ mi / 145+ km)** — *Wyoming Rockies, USA*  
  Rugged, self-supported high-altitude thru-hike penetrating America's premier glaciated alpine wilderness. Navigates off-trail boulder-hopping, Class 2/3 talus scrambling, and glacial moraines through **Titcomb Basin (10,600 ft)**, **Knapsack Col (12,205 ft / 3,720m)**, Gannett Peak glacial backcountry, and the sheer granite spires of the **Cirque of the Towers** via Texas Pass (11,480 ft).

### 2. Remote Wilderness High-Altitude Solos (Wyoming)
* **Gannett Peak Solo (13,804 ft / 4,207 m)** — *Wyoming State Highpoint*  
  Solo ascent of the most remote state highpoint in the lower 48. Demands a ~45–50 mile self-supported round-trip backcountry trek through the wild Wind River Range, navigating complex glaciated terrain (Gooseneck / Dinwoody Glacier), bergschrund crossings, and loose summit snow arêtes with zero external support.
* **Fremont Peak Solo (13,745 ft / 4,189 m)** — *Wind River Range*  
  Solo ascent of the towering granite mass rising above Titcomb Basin. Long wilderness approaches, Class 3/4 alpine scrambling, and sustained high-elevation exposure.
* **Grand Teton Solo (13,775 ft / 4,198 m)** — *Grand Teton National Park*  
  Solitary ascent of the iconic cathedral peak of the American Rockies, navigating steep couloirs, exposed knife-edges, and technical upper granite pitches.
* **Middle Teton Solo (12,804 ft / 3,903 m)** — *Grand Teton National Park*  
  Fast solo ascent via Southwest Couloir and glaciated alpine moraines.

* **Snow Peaks 50 (Snow50) Ultra Marathon (50 Miles · ~14,000 ft gain · 14h 44m 57s)** — *Wasatch Mountains, Utah*

  One of the world's most demanding, technical singletrack mountain biking descents. Starting above timberline at **Burro Pass (11,150 ft / 3,398m)** in the alpine snowmelt of the La Sal Mountains, traversing through Hazard County forest, Kokopelli trail, Upper/Lower Porcupine Singletrack (UPS/LPS), and down the exposed sandstone cliffs of Porcupine Rim to the **Colorado River (4,000 ft / 1,219m)**. Demands extreme bike handling under deep aerobic exhaustion, arm-pump endurance, and high mental presence over non-stop technical rock drops, slickrock shelves, and severe desert temperature swings.
* **La Sal Range Alpine Summits & Skyline Linkup (Moab, Utah)**  
  Alpine mastery across Utah's second highest mountain range, rising 8,000 vertical feet directly out of Moab's red rock desert. Completed the classic **La Sal Triple Crown**: linking **Mount Peale (12,721 ft / 3,877m — range highpoint)**, **Mount Mellenthin (12,645 ft / 3,854m)**, and **Mount Tukuhnikivatz (12,482 ft / 3,805m)** across steep talus basins, unstable volcanic porphyry ridges, and exposed Class 3/4 scrambling with panoramic views spanning Canyonlands and Arches National Parks.
* **Provo Ultimate Ridge Linkup (PURL)**  
  One of the most grueling unofficial mountain linkups in North America: traversing ~24–30 miles with 12,000–15,000+ vertical feet of sustained Class 3/4 ridgeline scrambling across the peaks overlooking Utah Valley.
* **Alpine Ridge Traverse (ART)** — 22 miles · 10,000 ft gain in 9h 40m across jagged American Fork and Little Cottonwood ridgelines.
* **Wasatch Ultimate Ridge Linkup (WURL) Horseshoe** — 36 miles · 21,000+ ft gain · 21 peaks of non-stop Class 3/4 ridgeline scrambling encircling Little Cottonwood Canyon. *(Active October Launch Window: Undergoing reduced-volume taper protocol through October 2; pre-hydration loading complete; Cardiff Pass & Catherine Pass caches staged).*
* **Mount Whitney Needles Crest Traverse (High Sierra)** — Continuous Class 3/4 ridge linking Discovery Pinnacle (13,750 ft), Mount Muir (14,025 ft), S'brutal Tower, and Keeler Needle.
* **Mount Whitney Double Summit in One Week** — Summited Mount Whitney twice in 7 days: via the 22.4-mile trail and the technical 40° Mountaineers Route chimney.
* **Yosemite High-Country Marathon** — 26.6 miles · 8,500 ft gain linking Sentinel Dome, Clouds Rest, and Half Dome in 9h 25m.
