Partnership

Expedition Partners

Brands and allies who cover expedition costs—gear, communications, and logistics—for the 2028 Great Himalayan Trail pilgrimage. Donor gifts go to MedOnMt’s meditation sanctuary, not to the walk.

This page is for expedition support, not naming rights on the story. There are no logos in the site hero or pilgrimage narrative. Recognition is modest and offered with gratitude.

What Partners Cover

Expedition Partners cover expedition costs. Donor gifts go to the meditation center and sanctuary. See the Sanctuary transparency section.

  • Gear & apparel

    Shelter, footwear, shells, hardware for high cols.

  • Communications

    Satellite messaging and safety hardware for remote sectors.

  • Logistics

    Permits, resupply, travel, and expedition operating costs.

Ways to Help

In-Kind Support & Shared Intentions

In-kind gear & field notes

Equip the pilgrimage for 2027 preparation objectives and the 2028 High Route. Field notes from the trail may be shared as secondary material—quietly, and never as branding on the public story pages.

Brand donation matching

Where a brand matches patron gifts toward the sanctuary, that matching is welcomed as an act that multiplies the pilgrimage’s purpose. Details are still being discerned.

Post-expedition gear auction

Trail-used equipment may later be offered at auction, with proceeds directed to the sanctuary. This remains an intention for after the walk, not a current sale.

With Gratitude

Modest Recognition

Partners who enable the walk may be thanked by name in a quiet list on this page and, when appropriate, mentioned in field dispatches. We do not place brand logos on the home hero, the sanctuary story, or the pilgrimage narrative.

Partners will be thanked here

Names will appear with consent once partnerships are confirmed. Anonymous support is welcome.

Context

2027 Preparation Objectives

Before Nepal, Aadesh will climb and walk a set of American alpine objectives (Shasta, Rainier, Granite Peak, Stairway to Heaven, Mjölnir) as preparation for a safe solo High Route. Partners who equip those days help ready the pilgrimage—not a brand showcase.

Movement record for those who want it: Strava. Contemplative dispatches: Substack.

Partner Inquiry
