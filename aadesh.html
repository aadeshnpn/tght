Kathmandu → Wasatch Range · Machine Learning Researcher & Alpinist

Aadesh Neupane

High-Altitude Endurance Athlete · Founder, MedOnMt · Contemplative Researcher

Born in Nepal under the shadow of the Himalayas and forged across the jagged granite knife-edges of the Wasatch Range, Wind River Range, Tetons, High Sierra, and the La Sal Range, Aadesh Neupane operates at the rare intersection of elite mountain endurance, cognitive science, and Eastern contemplative tradition.

As a published researcher in machine learning and human behavior and the founder of MedOnMt (Meditate On Mountain), Aadesh approaches extreme alpine endeavors as both an engineering optimization challenge and a moving meditation. His athletic profile combines remote wilderness solos (Gannett Peak, Fremont Peak, Grand & Middle Teton), big-wall sport climbing (the 23-pitch Squawstruck), technical ice climbing (such as the 10-pitch Stairway to Heaven), mountain ultramarathon endurance (Snow Peaks 50 / Snow50), iconic mountain bike endurance (The Whole Enchilada in Moab), high-altitude alpine linkups in the La Sal Mountains (Mount Peale, Tukuhnikivatz, Mellenthin), high-altitude thru-hikes (Manaslu Circuit, Tilicho Lake via Mesokanta Pass, Upper Mustang, Gosaikunda, Wind Rivers), Wasatch winter first ascents, and all-season summit mastery.
