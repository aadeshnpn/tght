Founder, MedOnMt · Pilgrim of the 2028 High Route

I am crossing the Himalayas solo in Spring 2028 to help lay the foundation for a permanent meditation sanctuary. The Great Himalayan Trail High Route—1,700 kilometers of high cols, silence, and effort—is the pilgrimage that carries that vision into the world.

The Return

Kathmandu Roots · Wasatch Granite

Born in Nepal under the shadow of the Himalayas, Aadesh grew up where mountains are living teachers and Buddhist practice is part of the air. Rooted now in Utah’s Wasatch Range, he works at the meeting of machine learning, cognitive science, and Theravada meditation—and is stepping more fully into championing stillness through MedOnMt.

The 2028 High Route is a homecoming: navigating his native terrain in a light, mindful, solo style—so the pilgrimage can raise a sanctuary that outlasts any single crossing.

Pali Canon & Contemplative Practice

Trained in Theravada traditions emphasizing the Four Noble Truths, vipassana, and metta. Leads high-altitude wilderness retreats across the Wasatch and Uintas.

Movement as Practice

Climbing, long ridge days, and trail running as ways of stripping distraction—preparation for 140 days of walking meditation on the High Route.

Sovereign Solo Style

As a Nepali citizen, Aadesh can traverse restricted border sectors independently—Kanchenjunga, Manaslu, Upper Mustang, Upper Dolpo, Humla—without foreign-agency escort requirements that constrain many expeditions.

The Aim

The Meditation Sanctuary

The pilgrimage’s main purpose is to raise a permanent place of practice. The sanctuary is envisioned most likely below a mountain, with a main house, two guest houses, and a meditation hall designed to be self-sustaining—hall first, then the other buildings gradually. Name and exact location are still being discerned.

Partners cover the walk; donor gifts build the sanctuary. We are not accepting gifts online yet. If you’d like to be among the first patrons, write to aadesh@medonmt.org.

Preparation · Not Trophies

Why This Solo Route Is Possible

Athletic credentials matter here as reassurance: a long solo high route demands proven wilderness judgment, altitude experience, and technical skill. These climbs and circuits are offered as evidence of readiness—not as a sponsor résumé.

Nepal & Wilderness Circuits

Manaslu Circuit

Solo · Larkya La 5,106m

Remote high-altitude circuit through Tibetan-border enclaves and glaciated moraines.

Tilicho via Mesokanta

Solo · Mesokanto La 5,121m

Off-teahouse alpine traverse to Tilicho Lake and into Mustang’s Kali Gandaki.

Upper Mustang Circuit

Solo · Kingdom of Lo

High-desert restricted plateau along the Tibetan border.

Gosaikunda Circuit

Solo · Laurebina 4,610m

Sacred lakes and high ridges linking Langtang and Helambu.

Wind River Thru-Hike

90+ mi · Wyoming Rockies

Self-supported alpine wilderness via Titcomb, Knapsack Col, Cirque of the Towers.

Snow Peaks 50

50 mi · 14h 44m · Wasatch

Mountain ultra finish confirming all-day vertical endurance.

Remote Solos & Technical Lines
