MedOnMt · The Heart of the Pilgrimage

A Permanent Meditation Sanctuary

In Spring 2028, Aadesh Neupane will walk the Great Himalayan Trail High Route alone—not as a feat to watch, but as a pilgrimage to help raise a lasting place of stillness. The trail is the means. The sanctuary is the aim.

MedOnMt (Meditate On Mountain) is a registered US nonprofit. We are not accepting gifts online yet. If you’d like to be among the first patrons, write to aadesh@medonmt.org, or follow the field dispatches.

Why the Trail

The Walk That Builds the Place

Every kilometer of the Nepal High Route is offered in service of a permanent meditation sanctuary—a physical refuge rooted in Himalayan and Theravada wisdom, where people can practice silence, attention, and care for one another long after the expedition ends.

Expedition Partners cover the costs of the walk (gear, communications, logistics). Donor gifts go to the meditation sanctuary. See also our partners page.

The Envisioned Place

A Self-Sustaining Sanctuary, Built in Phases

The sanctuary is envisioned most likely to sit below a mountain. Planned buildings include a main house, two guest houses, and a meditation hall, designed to be self-sustaining. The work is phased: the meditation hall is built first, then the other buildings follow gradually.
